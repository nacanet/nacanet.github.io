**T-shirts to breathe in.**
Inspiratee is a t-shirt brand with messages of calm and mindfulness: *breathe in peace, breathe out tension*.

## The brand

- T-shirts in four colours: sage, sand, cream and white.
- Original designs: *Breathe in peace*, *Find your inner peace* and *Stay present*.
- A palette inspired by the t-shirts themselves: sage, sand and charcoal.

## The shop

A custom-built online shop, minimal and designed for mobile:

- Product catalogue by colour, with a size selector.
- Persistent cart.
- Multi-step checkout.
- Newsletter.

Technologies: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Zustand and Medusa.

## Status

The shop is **under construction**: catalogue, cart and step-by-step checkout are done; the payment gateway arrives in the next phase.
